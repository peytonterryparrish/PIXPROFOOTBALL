# PIX Football v0.3.0 - "Playbook"

The full playbook arrives: a real play-call screen with formations, sets and play art, audibles and hot routes at the line, penalties you can accept or decline, college rules from kickoff to overtime, and a CPU coach that calls a smarter game.

## Headline features
- **Full play-call screen** - Formation, then Set, then three play cards per page, each drawn from the play itself (routes with arrows, blocking T's, run paths, defensive zones, blitz arrows). Tabs for **Playbook**, **Ask Coach** (the coach picks plays for the down, distance, field position, score and clock and tells you why), **Recent** and **Special Teams**. Filter by Run / Pass / Play Action / Screen (Zone / Man / Blitz on defense), flip any play, call a timeout without leaving the screen. The play clock keeps ticking while you choose.
- **More alignments** - Shotgun Doubles and Bunch, Singleback Ace, I-Form Slot, Pistol Trips: 371 plays in all.
- **At the line** - five **audibles** per formation, **hot routes** (streak, fade, slant, out, in, curl, block), **flip the play**, **motion**, **slide protection**. On defense: **coverage audibles** (Cover 2 / 3 / 1 / 0 / 4), **press / off**, **line pinch / spread**, **linebacker shifts**, **show blitz / back off**, and B cycles the defender you control. What you set up is what is snapped.
- **Penalties** - the flag panel names the foul, team, player number and yardage, shows the referee's announcement, and lets you **Accept** or **Decline** with the resulting down and spot for each. False starts, offsides and delay of game (let the play clock run out) stop the play at once.
- **Officials and coaches** - a seven-man crew follows the play and signals touchdowns, first downs, incompletions, flags, timeouts and safeties; coaches pace both sidelines in team colours.
- **Smarter CPU** - situational play calling (down, distance, field zone, score, clock, coach tendencies), a hurry-up offense with spikes and sideline routes, and defenses that match your personnel and blitz as often as their coach is aggressive.
- **College rules in a real game** - two feet in bounds, the clock stops on first downs, wider hash marks, tries from the 3, and alternating overtime from the 25.

In the field the v0.2 controls are unchanged (throw with A / B / X / Y / LB / RB, carrier moves, tackle with A, switch with B).

## Fixes
- The scorebug no longer shows through the final screen or other full-screen panels.
- Event banners are opaque and readable.
- Receiver buttons never overlap each other or leave the screen.
- Tackles and engaged blocks line up better: the players are pulled together at contact and face each other.
- Pro and college hash marks: the ball is spotted on the hash of the rule set in use.

## Known issues
- The stadium is painted with pro hash marks; college games draw their own ticks on top.
- Audibles cannot be edited in a menu yet.
- A hot route makes the receiver the quarterback's first read, but he still throws to whoever opens first.
- Tackle contact is better but still not physics-blended.

## What's next - v0.4.0 "2K5 feel pass"
Physics-blended tackles, juke / spin / stiff-arm / truck polish, lead and touch passing, catching in traffic.

## How to play
Download `PIXFootball_v0.3.0_Windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `PIXFootball.exe`. Plug in an Xbox controller before launching for the best experience. Requires Windows 10/11 64-bit and a DirectX 12 GPU.
