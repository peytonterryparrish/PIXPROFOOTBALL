# PIX Football v0.2.0 — "Kickoff"

A full game of football against the CPU. Pick two teams from the league, win the coin toss, and play 11-on-11 from the opening kickoff to the final whistle, with a real game clock, scoring, quarters, halftime and a final score screen.

## Headline features
- **Play Now** — choose home and away from all 32 pro teams (crest, colours and team rating shown live), quarter length (2 / 3 / 5 / 8 / 15 minutes), pro or college rules, CPU difficulty (Rookie / Pro / All-Pro) and which side you play.
- **A complete game** — coin toss (call it, then receive or defer), kickoffs, drives, touchdowns, extra point or two-point try, field goals, punts, safeties, turnovers, penalties, two-minute warning, timeouts, quarters with ends swapping, halftime, overtime and a final score screen with box score and team stats. The game clock is accelerated: a 5-minute quarter plays about 60-70 snaps per game.
- **22 players on the field** — linemen, backers, backs and receivers in three body sizes, in team colours (home in the primary colour, visitors in white with the team colour as trim, helmets in the team colour), with their own stances, blocks, routes, catches, throws, handoffs, tackles and celebrations.
- **The ball** — snapped, thrown in a spiral, carried tucked under the arm, kicked end over end, fumbled and bouncing.
- **Call your plays** — a pre-snap play call panel with four to six plays on A / B / X / Y / LB / RB for your situation (run, pass, blitz, coverage, punt, field goal, kickoff, PAT / two-point).
- **2K-style control** — you are the quarterback pre-snap, then the ball carrier after a handoff or catch; on defense you control the player nearest the action. A violet ring marks your player.
- **Broadcast presentation** — scorebug (crests, score, quarter, clock, play clock, down and distance, timeouts, possession), a blue line of scrimmage and yellow first-down line on the grass, banners for TOUCHDOWN / FIRST DOWN / FIELD GOAL / INTERCEPTION / FUMBLE / SAFETY / TURNOVER ON DOWNS, whistles, hits, catches, kicks, crowd roars and boos.
- **Brighter night game** and a regulation-size midfield logo.

## Controls (Xbox controller; keyboard in brackets)
| Moment | Control |
|---|---|
| Move / sprint | Left stick (WASD) / RT (Shift) |
| Choose a play | A / B / X / Y / LB / RB (Space / F / C / R / Z / X) |
| Snap the ball | A |
| Change the play before the snap | Y |
| Call a timeout | D-pad down (T) |
| Throw to a receiver | Tap its button for a bullet, hold for touch / lob; right stick nudges the lead; D-pad up throws it away |
| Ball carrier | A hurdle, B spin, X dive, Y truck, LB / RB stiff-arm, flick right stick to juke, LT protect the ball |
| Defense | A tackle, X dive, Y jump / swat, B switch to the player nearest the ball |
| Camera | View (Tab): broadcast, behind the player, first person |
| Pause | Menu (Esc) |

## Fixes
- The midfield logo is regulation size (about 11 yards across).
- The night game is brighter and reads like a TV broadcast.
- The ball is in the players' hands.

## Known issues
- No kicking motion-capture exists in the asset set: kicks and punts use a procedural leg swing.
- Tackle and block animations are paired mocap played in place (the engine owns the player positions), so contact can look a little off.
- Playbook screen is a quick 4-6 play menu; the full playbook arrives in v0.3.
- Crowd is still static.

## What's next — v0.3.0
The full playbook (formations, sets, hot routes and audibles), substitutions and depth chart, replays and the pause-menu stats.

## How to play
Download `PIXFootball_v0.2.0_Windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `PIXFootball.exe`. Plug in an Xbox controller before launching for the best experience. Requires Windows 10/11 64-bit and a DirectX 12 GPU.
