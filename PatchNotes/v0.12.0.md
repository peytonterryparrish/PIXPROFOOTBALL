# PIX Football v0.12.0 - "Realism I"

**A movement and rules release.** This is the first slice of your realism brief:
- feet that stay planted in blocks and tackles;
- bodies that lean into starts and stops;
- the new MoCap Online running pack;
- penalties that come from what the players actually do;
- 25 seconds between plays.

It also covers the three notes you added during the round: robotic movement, bodies and pads, and heads, hips and helmets. Every item and its numbers are in `Docs/QA/v0120/STATUS.md`.

## On the field
- **Blocks and tackles:** linemen and tacklers keep their feet planted and move by stepping. Planted-foot sliding is down from 1.9 m/s to 0.28 m/s on average. Hands go to the other man's pads in about 79 % of engaged moments (was 12 %).
- **Walk to the line:** players use the time before the snap and walk or jog in, instead of gliding over at 5 m/s.
- **Time between plays:** 25 seconds from whistle to snap by default (was 16). You can change it in Settings > Gameplay. A game still has about the same number of plays.
- **Pre-snap rules:** the game checks the formation: 7 on the line, who is eligible, covered receivers, a full second set after a shift, and one man in motion who is not moving toward the line. False starts, offside, encroachment, illegal formation, illegal shift, illegal motion and delay of game now come from what a player actually does. There are no more random rolls. That's about 2.3 pre-snap flags per team per game; false start is the most common.

## Performance
Built player, 2560 x 1440 High, 80 s of live play on an RTX 5090 laptop: **144.6 FPS on average and 110.7 for the slowest 1 %** (v0.11.0: 144.7 / 116.1), no errors. In a full watched game against the same game on v0.11, sudden hip snaps went from 2,147 to 862, players running facing the wrong way from 48 to 30, and the camera errors are gone.

## Known issues
- In blocks, the feet sometimes read a few centimetres into the turf in the audit. Floating in blocks is fixed: back to the v0.11 level or lower.
- Wrap tackles still drift at the start of the hit, and jogging still slides a little (0.18 m/s, target 0.10).
- The MoCap Online pack has no true sprint, so top speed reuses its fastest run. Its stop and turn moves slid too much to use.
- Players don't yet look as real as the Burrow model (see above).
- The player who jumps early on a false start lurches in the simulation, but the camera doesn't show it yet.
- Covered tight ends are still thrown to. Roughing the passer and offensive holding are called too often.
