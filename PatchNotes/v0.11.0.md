# PIX Football v0.11.0 - "Broadcast"

**This is a watching release.** You asked to watch CPU against CPU matches on the broadcast camera and judge the engine before controller input comes back, so every game is now a watch game, and this release answers your v0.10.0 notes with that in mind: the players no longer sink, vanish or fold on kickoffs, the camera keeps the kick in the picture, the game keeps the same 22 bodies from play to play (huddle, walk, substitutions, timeouts), the quarterback and the running backs move like their positions, the sound is a real stadium with music, and the menus are the new Tile Hub.

## Menus
The new **Tile Hub Modern** menus are the default: home, play, settings, team select, matchup, the 29 franchise pages and the match HUD, in Barlow with gold on charcoal. Controller navigation was checked on 42 screens. College and Road to Heisman are not in the menus.

## Sound
Cinematic instrumental music replaces the songs: a menu playlist, a pre-game bed, a quiet underscore between plays, a tense one in the two-minute drill, and stingers for touchdowns, field goals, turnovers and the final whistle. On the field, snaps, blocks, sheds, throws, catches, hits, pile-ons and cleats on the turf all make sound (8 event types before, 32 now, plus every contact), and the crowd has ten layers with reactions to sacks, incompletions, penalties, big hits and scores. The crowd in the stands now sways, stands and slumps.

## Performance
Built player, 2560 x 1440 High, 80 s of live play on an RTX 5090 laptop: **144.7 FPS on average and 116.1 for the slowest 1 %** (v0.10.0: 148.5 / 114.5), no errors. A full 3-minute-quarter watch game (184 plays) on the integrated tree shows no player sunk under the turf and no body buried when the camera returns (v0.10: 705 sunk-body hits in one game); players hovering a few centimetres in their pre-snap stance is the main thing the audit still flags.

## Known issues
- **Watch mode only.** Controller input is off by default, and the play-call and pre-snap panels for a user game have not been reworked yet.
- **Music needs an ear check.** The tracks were chosen from instrument stems and measured, not listened to: if you hear a voice in `menu_calm`, `menu_house` or the halftime loop, tell us which.
- **No cadence voice** ("hut, hut") because there is no source recording, **replays are silent**, and the crowd animation is hard to see from the broadcast camera.
- **Stance feet** are flagged about 10 cm up for much of the pre-snap by the audit (screenshots look normal). **Wrap tackles** still slide at the start of the hit (1.98 m/s), and **jogging and shuffling** still skate a little (0.17 / 0.21 m/s).
- **Players stand still after the whistle** (about 10 a play) and the time between snaps is 16 s; the 25 s default is coming. Only 0.6 plays a game end out of bounds (NFL about 7) because the passing game is too middle-heavy.
- **Quarterback:** the kneel and spike are not verified, the play-action fake reaches too deep, a hit stumble recovers stiffly, and some under-center throws still scissor their feet. 28 % of throws land more than 1 m from the receiver.
- **Players are small** on the broadcast camera: 50-60 px tall on a normal play and 34-56 px on a kickoff (22 players across 60 m). Director shots for celebrations, the sideline QB and touchbacks are not done.
- **Menus:** the ticker's NEWS block renders dark, two franchise pages need four presses, there is no hero artwork yet, and Online, Superstar, Trophy Room and Mini Games are locked tiles.
- Everything under Known issues in v0.10.0 that is not named above still applies.
