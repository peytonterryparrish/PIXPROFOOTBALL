# PIX Football v0.9.1 - "Frame Rate"

**Same game, same look, much smoother.** v0.9.0 dropped to about 75-80 fps with dips into the 50s at 1440p High. v0.9.1 fixes the cause.

## What changed
- **Players are now animated on the graphics card.** The player bodies were being deformed on the processor and re-sent to the graphics card every frame. Moving that work to the GPU roughly halves the graphics time in a match.
- **DLSS / FSR now actually turn on.** The "Auto" upscaler said DLSS was active, but a settings clash kept it off. It now really runs (DLSS Quality on RTX cards, FSR 2 elsewhere).
- Small clean-ups in the per-frame level-of-detail code.

## Frame rate (RTX 5090 Laptop, 2560 x 1440, High, night game, live play)
| | average | 1% low |
|---|---|---|
| v0.9.0 | 82 fps | 50 fps |
| v0.9.1 | 145 fps | 82 fps |

Measured with nothing else running. If other heavy programs share the CPU, expect brief dips right after kickoffs and tackles.

## Known issues
- Short hitches can still happen at the end of a play (pile-ups, close-ups) when the processor is busy with other programs.
- Everything listed under Known issues in v0.9.0 still applies.

## What's next
User playtest of v0.9.x, then v0.10.0 "Motion Matching" (new running animations).

Send notes with the version (0.9.1), what you were doing and, for a visual bug, a screenshot. `PIXFootball.exe -pixAutotest` writes screenshots and frame rates to `Logs/autotest`.
