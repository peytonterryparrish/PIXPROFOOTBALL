# PIX Football v0.13.0 - "Mocap"

**A motion-capture release.** You said "so much motion capture is missing" and asked for at least 300 new clips, the quarterback's clips especially. So this round started by checking every mocap file you supplied against what the game actually plays, and ended with a game you can watch:
- **945 different mocap clips** played in one watched game (v0.12: 364);
- **649 of them new** (target 300);
- **209 of the new ones are quarterback clips** (v0.12: about 10 QB clips in a game).

The count comes from a new recorder that logs every clip a game plays, not from what was imported. Every item and its numbers are in `Docs/QA/v0130/STATUS.md`. The file-by-file inventory is in `Docs/QA/v0130/MOCAP_COVERAGE.md`.

## On the field
- **Tackles and catches:** 159 two-player contact pairs (was 113), including contested catches and strip attempts.
- **Huddles and sidelines:** more varied idles, stretches, conversations and celebrations.
- **Facing:** players facing the wrong way while standing: 1,385 -> 55 a game. Most of the old count was the audit misreading players in the huddle.

## Performance
Built player, 2560 x 1440 High, 80 s of live play on an RTX 5090 laptop: **145.5 FPS on average and 113.0 for the slowest 1 %** (v0.12.0: 144.6 / 110.7), no errors. More than twice the animation variety costs no frame rate.

## Known issues
- **QB moves still on keyed poses:** drops, scrambles, kneels, slides and sneaks. Fitted to the game's drop distance, the drop takes slid, so the old drops stay for now. The kneel, slide and sneak takes in the files can't be used (the actor never actually kneels or slides).
- **QB stance:** right 91 % of the time (97 % midway through the round). The hand-off starts a moment before the exchange.
- **Blocking:** the line's engage and cut-block takes all failed the clean-up checks. Blocks use the pass-set, push and fail takes instead.
- **Still wrong in the audit:** hip jumps (+18 %), players overlapping while standing or blocking (16 -> 35), camera jumps (+13 %).
- **Bodies:** closer to the R1 reference body, but not there yet. The cloth detail doesn't move with the body, the shoulders are still a little wide, and arms stretch when raised. In close-ups the side panel of the pants reads as a flat grey patch. As always, only that body is used as a reference, never a real face.
- **Not imported:** three Asset Store movement packs (MC Core Motion, Running Motions Pack 2, Movement Animset Pro).
