# PIX Football v0.8.0 - "Franchise"

**Career Mode is here.** Take over one of the 32 pro teams and run it for as many seasons as you like: set the depth chart, manage the cap, re-sign, trade, bid in free agency, scout and draft, and **play your own games in the real match** while the rest of the league is simulated around you. Player abilities, morale and your coaches' tendencies now matter on the field too.

## How to start a career
1. Main menu, **CAREER** tile (HOME tab of the hub).
2. **NEW CAREER**: pick your team's crest, the difficulty, the quarter length (your games use it), the league (**standard** = the exact ratings you see in the exhibition, **fresh** = a random league) and one of five save slots.
3. You land in the **Franchise hub**. In the preseason press **START THE SEASON** (the game tells you what blocks you: roster over 53, over the cap, and offers to fix it for you).
4. Week by week: **PLAY GAME** (the match scene, your quarter length) or **SIM GAME**. After a played game you come back to the hub with the **box score**; **FINISH WEEK** simulates the rest of the league. **SIM OPTIONS** skips to week N, the playoffs, the offseason or a whole season.
5. The offseason has eight steps (awards, retirements, re-sign, scouting, draft, rookie signing, free agency, training camp). Each shows what you can do and what blocks the next step. The career saves after every week, phase and draft pick. **CONTINUE** on the CAREER tile picks up the newest save.

## What is in Career Mode
- **HOME**: your next opponent (ratings, record, venue, weather), standings, owner goals, cap and roster status, inbox, the league wire.
- **TEAM**: roster with a photoreal player card (ratings, abilities with stars, contract, morale), depth chart editor (only eligible players, auto sort), injuries, practice squad (16), injured reserve, waivers.
- **LEAGUE**: schedule and box scores, standings and power rankings, playoff picture, leaders, awards and All-Pro teams, record book, trophy case, Hall of Fame, news.
- **OFFICE**: cap and contracts (restructure, release with a dead-money preview, franchise tag, auto-fix), re-sign negotiations (what he wants, how he feels), free agency (14 bidding days, players weigh money, contender status, role and loyalty), trades (value meter, ask what it takes, CPU offers, deadline after week 9), coaching staff (hire and fire, scheme fit), owner trust and your legacy score.
- **The draft**: spend scouting points to sharpen your board, star targets, watch the live pick list with grades, trade up, or let the auto pick run. Rookies sign on the rookie scale.
- **Franchise rules**: 53-man roster, cap 255M in year one (grows 5 % a year), dead money, contracts, injuries, retirements, Hall of Fame, a ten-season league that stays stable (tested over 60 simulated seasons).

## Abilities in the game
There are 41 abilities with one to three stars. They are not decoration: the match reads them. Examples at three stars (measured over thousands of plays, details in `Docs/QA/v080/sim_abilities.md`):
| Ability | What it does in the match |
|---|---|
| Ankle Shaker | juke success 44.6 % -> 50.9 % |
| Whirlwind | spin success 29.9 % -> 41.2 % |
| Money Down | quarterback aim error on third down -21 % |
| Cool Under Fire | aim error under pressure -12.7 % |
| Escape Artist | sacks on long dropbacks 32.0 % -> 25.7 % |
| Sure Tackler | missed tackles 8.2 % -> 6.2 % |
| Ball Hawk | interceptions per attempt 2.5 % -> 4.7 % |
| Ice In The Veins | 51-yard field goal in a tied fourth quarter 71 % -> 77 % |
| Return Ace | kickoff return +7 %, punt return +9 % |
| Gap Plugger | yards per carry against his front 3.50 -> 2.67 |
The player card shows the effect sizes the match really applies. Morale moves every rating by up to two points, and the coaches' tendencies (blitz, man coverage, deep shots, no-huddle tempo) change how the CPU calls plays: a game between two spread teams runs about 30 more plays than one between two power-run teams. League scoring stays football-like (27.6 points a team over 6,000 games).

## Performance
Built player, 2364x1330 High on an RTX 5090 laptop: live play in a career game 111 FPS average (68 FPS 1 %-low, 34 minimum); exhibition match 109 average (69 1 %-low); free roam 147. The franchise screens render live player portraits; no frame problems seen. A season simulates in about 1.5 s.

## Known issues
- **Draft classes are synthetic** until v0.9 (the 138-school college world is not part of the career yet), so prospects have schools but no college stats.
- **No in-match injuries**: injuries are rolled by the season after your game (the Iron Man ability reduces that roll).
- Career settings (auto depth chart, auto IR, deadline week, free agency days, injuries on / off) have defaults and no screen yet.
- A regular-season game that ends level is a tie; the playoffs never end level.
- Some ability descriptions are cut off at the card width.
- A fresh draft board is blurry on purpose: spend scouting points before you trust the order.
- Everything listed under Known issues in v0.7.0 about animation and stadium presentation still applies.
