# PIX Football v0.4.0 - "Contact"

The 2K5 feel pass. Tackles now look and feel like football: players meet where the motion capture says they should, wrap, drive, drag each other and go down in a pile; runners lean into their cuts and plant their feet; the quarterback reads the receiver you hot-routed; and big hits shake the camera and your controller. The game also runs about 75 percent faster.

## Headline features
- **Real tackles** - every tackle, sack and big hit now plays an authored two-player take aligned to the actual positions of the players: the tackler comes in from where he really came from (front, side or behind), the contact lands on the carrier, and the pair hits the turf together. Wrap-ups, low dives, big hits, arm tackles that miss, drags and sacks all look different.
- **Drags and gang tackles** - a tackler who grabs a heavy runner hangs on while the runner churns forward; more defenders pile on when the tackle finally happens.
- **Ragdoll hits** - truck a defender, win a stiff arm, or pancake a lineman and the loser is thrown back by physics, tumbles, and gets up again (face up or face down, whichever he landed).
- **Ball-carrier polish** - runners bank into turns and lean into accelerations, the planted foot stays planted instead of skating, your stiff arm reaches for the defender, holding LT tucks the ball high and tight, and the spin is a real mocap spin.
- **Catching in traffic** - when a defender is on the receiver, both players go up for the ball.
- **Hot routes work** - the receiver you hot-route is now the quarterback's first read and he favours him when he is open.
- **You can feel it** - big hits shake the camera a little, your controller rumbles when your player is hit, sacks, trucks and catches (Settings: Controller rumble / Camera shake on hits), and hit sounds get heavier with the force of the hit.
- **Faster** - live play at 2560x1440 now averages about 107 frames per second on the test machine at the Ultra tier (it was about 61 in v0.3.0), roughly 75 percent faster. New graphics tiers (Low / Medium / High / Ultra) and a Render scale option in Settings.

## Controls
No changes. New in Settings: Graphics quality (Low / Medium / High / Ultra), Render scale (100 / 90 / 80 / 67 percent), Controller rumble, Camera shake on hits.

## Fixes
- Tackle animations no longer snap players into a pose when they start.
- Tacklers and carriers no longer sink into each other after the hit.
- Tackler and carrier were playing each other's animation: fixed.

## Known issues
- Pile-ups of three or more tacklers are approximate.
- Sideline toe-tap catches reuse the normal side-catch animation.
- DLSS / FSR are not in yet; Render scale uses the engine's dynamic resolution.

## What's next - v0.5.0 "Broadcast"
TV overlays, instant replay with a free camera and depth of field, halftime and postgame shows, a broadcast camera director, and first-person mode polish.

## How to play
Download `PIXFootball_v0.4.0_Windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `PIXFootball.exe`. Plug in an Xbox controller before launching for the best experience. Requires Windows 10/11 64-bit and a DirectX 12 GPU.
