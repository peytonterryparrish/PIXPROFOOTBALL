# PIX Football v0.9.0 - "Match Engine"

**The match itself got rebuilt.** This release answers your v0.8.0 playtest notes: runners no longer float, feet and knees point where the player is going, every position has a real stance, the quarterback has a proper snap, drop, pocket and throw, animations now connect to each other instead of popping, the game is slower and more natural, and the picture looks more high-end. College Network is paused so everything went into the engine; its unfinished screens are not in this build.

We audited the match engine, wrote down **448 issues** (list: `Docs/QA/v090/ENGINE_AUDIT_issues.csv`) and worked through them: **361 fixed, 54 partly fixed, 14 handed to a later release, 14 deliberately not done, 5 still open** (status of every row in `Docs/QA/v090/ENGINE_AUDIT_STATUS.md`).

## Locomotion (running)
- Running is now built from the mocap cycles at the right speed for each player, with the planted foot held on the turf, the sole pressed flat, and a short compression when the foot lands.
- Square knees, feet and ankles: knees track over the toes (stance deviation about 1.5 cm, was 5-7 cm), feet land flat within about 10 degrees of the line you run in (was 25-40 degrees turned out), no more cleats rolled onto their edges, no jittering ankles.
- Kickers, receivers and defenders stop sliding after the whistle and stop skating on cuts.
- Back-pedals and shuffles are planted too (they still use the old animation set, see Known issues).

## Stances for every position
- **Linemen:** real three-point and four-point stances with a hand on the turf (offense and defense), edge rushers lean toward the quarterback, goal-line fronts go down in four-point, the center holds the ball.
- **Receivers and corners:** staggered feet with the outside foot back, press corners square with hands up, off corners in a chair, safeties and linebackers have their own set.
- **Backs:** two-point stance with hands on thighs, fullbacks and goal-line backs one hand down.
- **Special teams:** kicker, punter, long snapper (wide base, head down), kickoff coverage runners staggered, receiving line with hands up, returner with hands out, holder on the kicker's side.
- Cadence: the head swivels, the player bounces and the quarterback drops his hips on the "hut".
- Offensive line goes to a two-point stance when the QB is in shotgun on a pass play.

## Quarterback
- **Under center:** hands under the center's hips, snap received into the belly, reverse pivot, 3 / 5 / 7 step drops that land on the planned spot with both feet on the ground (flight phase went from 51-63 % of the drop to 0).
- **Shotgun and pistol:** hands at the chest, snap caught, gather step and retreat.
- **Pocket and throws:** the passer steps into the throw, bullet / touch / lob each have their own arm, pump fakes, throw-away starts before the release, the ball spins about its long axis on passes and punts, end over end on kickoffs.
- Scramble, roll-out, kneel, spike and toss each have a real pose. Left-handed passers mirror correctly.
- **Slower, realistic timing:** the quarterback now takes 1.9 s (shotgun 3-step) to 3.7 s (shotgun 7-step) to release, throws fly on the NFL curve (a 40 yard throw takes about 2.35 s), arm strength is capped at 57 mph.

## Animation connections
- Players are drawn smoothly between simulation steps (no stutter on 144 Hz displays; frames with no movement 58 % -> 0 %).
- No more pre-snap teleports: shifts, flips and the huddle break are walked; the snap waits until everyone is set.
- Blocking: linemen play matched engagement pairs, run-block footwork, a hand-fight loop and a pancake finish; receivers and backs use a stalk block.
- Tackles: momentum carries through the hit, gang tacklers arrive and wrap, pile-ons and helping-up after the whistle, a QB hit after the throw reacts.
- Catching: the receiver reaches for the real ball, then holds and tucks it. Hand-offs pass the ball hand to hand.
- Rapid animation flips and sudden hip snaps are down (hips snapping more than 900 degrees per second: 3,715 -> 873 frames over 48 plays).

## Game sim
- Slower overall pace (see note 10), different speeds per line position, realistic acceleration and cutting limits, a fallen player gets up in 0.4 s at 40 % speed, tired players accelerate worse.
- Realistic pass flight, a fitted ball curve, releases that wait for the route and the drop, pass blocking with proper slide assignments, better zone and man coverage, run reads that sometimes misread, special teams penalties (roughing the kicker, running into the kicker, kick-catch interference), onside kicks that work about 11 % of the time.
- League scoring stays football-like: 26.2 points per team, 59.9 plays, 211 net passing yards and 135 rushing yards per team over 40 simulated games.

## Graphics
- 4-cascade soft shadows tuned to the range you actually see, floodlight shadows on High, foot shadows on every tier, lower sun.
- Players use the full-detail model wherever they are big on screen (the level of detail now follows size on screen, not distance), with a matching shadow so nothing pops.
- Sharper edges, 16x anisotropic filtering for the turf stripes and lines, richer turf blades at mid distance, cloud layer, cleaner grade with less grain and bloom, far-only depth of field.
- DLSS Quality is chosen automatically on RTX cards (FSR otherwise), the quality tier floors keep the shadows on lower tiers.
- Ball spin and bounce, officials who stay near the spot between plays, larger jersey numerals.

## Performance
Built player, 2560x1440 High on an RTX 5090 laptop, 80 s of live play: 75 FPS on average and 52 FPS for the slowest 1 % of frames (34 FPS minimum); free roam 113 FPS. That is lower than v0.8.0 (109 / 69 on the same machine): the new shadows, model detail near the camera and animation layers cost frame time. On a weaker card, lower the quality tier in SETTINGS. Getting the slowest frames back above 60 is the first thing on the list for the next release.

## Known issues
- **Frame rate dropped:** see Performance (1 %-low 52 FPS here, below the 60 target).
- **Sprint cycles still fly a little:** both feet are in the air about 42-53 % of a sprint cycle (it was 72 %); that is a property of the mocap takes. A sprint take with two ground contacts would fix it.
- **Hard cuts at a sprint:** the feet can turn out up to about 17 degrees on average (45 at the worst) because the foot cannot follow a cut as fast as the sim turns.
- **Back-pedals** still pop a little when a defender turns to run, and their cadence (1.4-2.0 steps per second) is slower than a real back-pedal. Shuffles and crossovers are on the old animation set. There are no start, stop or plant-and-cut clips yet (stops and cuts are handled by the gait and the foot plant, not by a clip).
- **Catch frame:** at the exact frame the simulation completes a catch the ball can still be about 0.6 m from the hand; the ball is pulled into the hands over the next 0.2 s.
- **Line stances** are slightly deeper than the textbook numbers (hips 0.68-0.72 m): with a hand on the turf the shoulder cannot be higher on this body.
- **Crowd** is still not animated; the level of detail switch is not cross-faded (a small pop at the edge distance); clouds do not scroll; the numeral outline is not thicker.
- **Sack rate** is 3.3 % of dropbacks (NFL about 6.5 %); Read Option runs average 2.4-3.2 yards; a zero blitz that gets beaten breaks for 40+ yards about one run in five; punts average 49 yards (NFL about 47); a pass play lasts about 5.8 s from snap to the end of the post-play wrap, a bit long.
- **Clip changes** per second went up for linemen and receivers (the connection layer plays clips where there were none); a few residual hip snaps remain at the start of get-off clips.
- **Left-handed quarterbacks** are mirrored but not tuned; off-balance and jump throws do not exist yet.
- The huddle break and the call-screen tick are tested through the audit path only; if the snap button ever does not respond after the break, tell us.
- College Network is paused (nothing of it is in the menus); the college logic from the repository is still there for v1.0.
- Everything listed under Known issues in v0.8.0 that is not about animation still applies.

## What's next
More of the same: grounded sprint, back-pedal and start / stop / cut animations, crowd animation, then **College Network** returns.
