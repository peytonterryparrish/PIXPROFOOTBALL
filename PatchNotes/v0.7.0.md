# PIX Football v0.7.0 - "Motion"

This release answers your 31 playtest notes from v0.6.0. Players now **have weight and finish their animations**, the **quarterback plays like a quarterback** (under center, drops, pocket, real throws, scrambles), the game is **about 12 % slower and easier to read**, field goals and kickoffs take their time, you can **see who you control on defense**, **hold LT for routes**, **call plays from a new strip along the bottom of the screen**, the menus are a **tile hub**, replays show **all 22 players**, and the whistle tells you **why a play ended**.

## Headline features
- **Quarterback**: see notes 4, 14, 15.
- **Route view**: hold LT at the line (or as the QB).
- **Play-call strip**: the sheet along the bottom of the field, on offense and defense.
- **Menu hub and matchup card**.
- **Kick meter is live**: your timing and aim now decide power and accuracy of your kicks.
- **Why-the-play-ended banner** on every whistle.
- **15 photoreal bodies**, shoulder pads that no longer tear, blade-level turf, cleaner dirt.

## What changed in the controls
| Where | Control |
|---|---|
| Offense at the line: see the routes | **Hold LT** (V) |
| Offense: call a timeout | **D-pad down** (T) (it was LT) |
| Defense / kicking / return teams: timeout | LT (V), unchanged |
| QB: throw to a receiver | **Hold** the receiver button (about 0.17 s) = **bullet**; **tap** = **touch / lob** (it was the other way round) |
| Play-call strip: move along the cards | D-pad left / right |
| Play-call strip: change group (formation, concept, play type, personnel) | D-pad up / down |
| Play-call strip: call the highlighted play | A (a suggested play is one press) |
| Play-call strip: back to SUGGESTED / clear search | B |
| Play-call strip: page the cards | LB / RB |
| Play-call strip: next / previous tab | RT / LT (Shift / V) |
| Play-call strip: type filter / flip the play | X / Y |
| Play-call strip: quick find by letter | right stick left / right (J / L) |
| Play-call strip: search by name | `/` on the keyboard |
| Play-call strip: timeout | R3 (Backspace) |
| Main menu: tabs | LB / RB; d-pad to every tile; B to go back |
| Kicks | A stops the timing cursor, the left stick steers the aim; on field goals, extra points and punts the first A snaps, the second kicks; on kickoffs one A kicks |
| Everything else | as in v0.6.0 |

## What to test
1. **QB flow**: snap under center, the drop, the pocket, a throw on the run, a scramble: does the ball always look like it is in his hand, does the release match the pass?
2. **Weight**: do running backs and receivers plant their feet? Any glide on back-pedals or lateral shuffles?
3. **Pace and kicks**: does the game feel right now (not too fast, not too slow)? Field goals, kickoffs, punts with the kick meter.
4. **Route view and call strip**: can you reach a play in a few presses with only the controller?
5. **Replays**: after a touchdown or a sack, do all 22 players appear?
6. **Menus**: the hub, the matchup card, the pause menu.
Send notes with the version (0.7.0), what you were doing and, for a visual bug, a screenshot (`-pixAutotest` writes screenshots to `Logs/autotest`).

## Performance
Live play at 2560x1440 High on an RTX 5090 laptop (built player): 107 FPS average, 70 FPS 1%-low (target 70), 37 FPS minimum; with the broadcast camera and layer 100 average, 72 1%-low; replays and shows 108 average, 72 1%-low. Heavy frames are close-ups of the photoreal players at night or in weather. On a weaker GPU pick a lower graphics tier or set Player models to Classic.

## Known issues
- Back-pedals below 3 and above 4 m/s still slide a little; the shuffle cornerbacks' right foot toes out too far.
- Not in yet: start / stop / plant-and-cut transition clips, left-handed quarterbacks, receivers turning their head to the ball, offensive line stance tells.
- Defensive line technique alignment is built but switched off until the blocking scheme is recalibrated.
- The jumbotron hangs over midfield, but the tunnel, the news studio, the roof and the light towers are not wired into the shows yet.
- Day games under the roof look dark in the stands; skin and eye shading are unchanged.
- The sleeve stripe reads as a wing on some kits and the shoulder number is cut by the sleeve cap on a few.
- At 4:3 and 21:9 the call strip keeps its design size; the menu ticker is clipped at its right end.
