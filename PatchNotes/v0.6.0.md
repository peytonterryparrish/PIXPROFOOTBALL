# PIX Football v0.6.0 - "Photoreal"

This is the release that answers your v0.5.0 playtest. The players are now **photoreal** (real-proportion bodies, faces, skin tones, helmets, gloves, numbers and names), the linemen and corners **face the right way in the right stance**, the game plays from a **2K8-style camera behind the quarterback**, the match engine moves and tackles more like football, and the stadium has **weather, time of day and floodlights**. A 500-issue QA pass sits behind it: **3,540 issues logged, 2,908 fixed** (`Docs/QA/CHECKLIST.md`).

## Headline features
- **Photoreal players**: three body types by position and weight, faces and skin tones from the player id (the same player always looks the same, also in replays), gloves, sleeves, wristbands, visors. Settings > Gameplay > Player models switches back to the old bodies if your machine struggles.
- **Uniforms**: your number on the chest, back and sleeves, your name on the back and the team name on the chest, in a colour chosen so navy and black kits stay readable; grass stains and mud build up through the game and show in replays; helmets scuff.
- **2K8 camera and scorebug**: bottom-bar scorebug (down and distance, ball spot, quarter, clock, play clock), gold stars under your eligible receivers and a ring under your player before the snap.
- **Coin toss on the field**: referee at midfield, two captains per team, call heads or tails.
- **Kicks**: timing and aim meter and a wind compass with mph on every kick (see Known issues).
- **Weather and time of day**: pick it on the team-select screen. Rain soaks the field and the uniforms, snow piles up through the game, floodlights cut through the haze.
- **Better football**: run game recalibrated (every run concept now gains ground, 3.9 yards per carry overall), pursuit, tackling, blocking and kicking tuned with measurements, 16 penalty types.

## What to test
1. **Stances and facing** - watch the line pre-snap from the new camera in both directions and on defense: does every lineman, linebacker and corner face the ball, in a believable stance? Any T-pose, floating or sliding player, any helmet that is not on a head?
2. **The camera** - does it feel like 2K8 / Madden behind the QB? Pull-back at the snap, the follow on handoffs and catches, defense, kicks. Settings > Camera has height, distance, zoom-out and field of view.
3. **Photoreal players** - faces, numbers and names on dark and light kits, dirt after a long game, helmets, gloves. Anything that looks wrong close up (replays are the best place to look).
4. **Weather and night games** - pick rain, snow, fog and night on the team-select screen: legibility of the HUD and the jerseys, performance.
5. **The engine** - do games look like football (speeds, tackles, pocket, run game, penalties, kicking with wind)? Report anything that looks like a bug with the quarter, down and distance.
6. **Performance** - note your GPU, resolution, quality tier and upscaler, and when it dips.
Send notes with the version (0.6.0), what you were doing and, for a visual bug, a screenshot (`-pixAutotest` writes screenshots to `Logs/autotest`).

## Performance
Live play at 2560x1440 High on an RTX 5090 laptop: 108 FPS average, 61 FPS 1%-low (target 70), 45 FPS minimum; broadcast camera and cinematics 100 average, 74 1%-low; replays and shows 120 average. On a weaker GPU pick a lower graphics tier or switch Player models to Classic.

## Known issues
- The kick meter and the wind are display-only for your kicks: the simulation reads them, but your button timing does not drive them yet.
- Photoreal heads are game-grade sculpts and the scalp hair is a simple shell; beards and stubble are switched off until proper hair cards exist.
- The home crest at midfield is low-resolution and looks blocky up close; dirt on white away jerseys can look like camouflage late in a game.
- A few stance takes are shared between positions; the formation gallery fails 5 of 9,914 checks (two lineman head heights a few centimetres over the limit, three body angles during a block and a handoff).
- 374 QA rows are still open (see the checklist), mostly polish.
- The glass UI redesign is in the files but switched off: this release uses the Classic UI.
- Ray-traced shadows and reflections and grass blade cards are not in yet.
