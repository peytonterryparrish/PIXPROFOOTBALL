# PIX Football v0.1.0 — "First Steps"

The first playable build. Walk out into a full pro stadium under the lights, pick up a player and feel the movement engine that everything else will be built on.

## Headline features
- **Free Roam** — take a player onto the field and run. Movement is momentum-based like the classic 2K football games: players build speed, carry weight through their cuts and can't change direction instantly. Sprint drains stamina.
- **Ball-carrier moves** — juke (flick the right stick), spin (B), stiff-arm (LB/RB), truck (Y), hurdle (A), dive (X), protect the ball (LT) and celebrate (D-pad up).
- **Three cameras** — broadcast sideline, behind-the-player and a first-person helmet cam. Press **View** to switch.
- **Night and day games** — floodlit night game with volumetric haze, or a sunny afternoon. Switch from the pause menu or D-pad down.
- **The PIX PRO FOOTBALL LEAGUE** — main menu, midfield, wall pads and video boards carry the new league shield. All 32 pro teams' uniforms are in; change teams from the pause menu.
- **A packed house** — more than 25,000 fans in the stands.
- **Original soundtrack** plays on the main menu.

## Controls (Xbox controller)
| Action | Button |
|---|---|
| Move | Left stick |
| Sprint | RT |
| Juke | Flick right stick left/right (or D-pad left/right) |
| Truck | Y / flick right stick up |
| Spin | B |
| Hurdle | A |
| Dive | X |
| Stiff-arm | LB / RB |
| Protect ball | LT / hold right stick down |
| Celebrate | D-pad up |
| Camera | View |
| Day/Night | D-pad down |
| Reset player | Click left stick |
| Pause | Menu |

Keyboard: WASD move, Shift sprint, Q/E juke, F spin, Space hurdle, C dive, R truck, Z/X stiff-arm, V protect, Tab camera, L lighting, Esc pause.

## Under the hood
- Complete football rules engine (pro and college rules), a playbook of roughly 195 offensive and 70 defensive plays, the 32-team pro league and 138-school college world, and the 22-player on-field simulation are already built and tested — v0.2.0 puts all 22 players on the field.

## Known issues
- The midfield logo is too large.
- Night lighting is slightly dim.
- The crowd doesn't animate yet.
- Spin uses a procedural turn (no mocap yet); walking is a slowed jog.
- The ball isn't in your hands yet, and you can run through goal posts.

## What's next — v0.2.0
11-on-11 football: snap, handoffs and passes, blocking, tackling, downs and the game clock — a full playable game against the CPU.

## How to play
Download `PIXFootball_v0.1.0_Windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `PIXFootball.exe`. Plug in an Xbox controller before launching for the best experience. Requires Windows 10/11 64-bit and a DirectX 12 GPU.
