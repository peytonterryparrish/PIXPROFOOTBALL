# PIX Football v0.5.0 - "Broadcast"

Your game now looks like it is on TV. Every play is recorded for **instant replay** with a free camera and depth of field; a **camera director** opens the game and the drives with sky-cam swoops and sideline angles and cuts to the carrier, the celebration, the coach and the crowd after the whistle; **TV graphics** (drive summaries, player stat lines, score cards, a play-by-play ticker) come and go as the game moves; **halftime and postgame shows** run the stats and a highlight reel; the crowd jumps in a wave on big home plays; and first person got a helmet, head bob and a look-to-the-ball assist.

## Headline features
- **Instant replay** - Pause, then **Instant Replay**. The last play is on a loop with a free camera: orbit, pan and zoom around any player (or the ball), drop to ground level, jump to preset angles (sideline, end zone, high, behind the QB, follow the ball), scrub and rewind, slow motion at half and quarter speed, and a depth of field that keeps your target sharp. A bar at the bottom shows where you are and where the key moment is. Press View to flip back through the last plays.
- **Automatic replays** after a touchdown, a turnover, a sack, a run or catch of 20+ yards or a big hit: two broadcast angles, slow motion through the key moment, the key player in focus. Press **A** to skip. (Settings > Broadcast > Automatic replays.)
- **Camera director** (Settings > Broadcast > **Cinematics: Full / Minimal / Off**) - a sky-cam swoop before the opening kickoff, establishing shots on new drives (sky cam, a low sideline angle on your quarterback, an end-zone view), a tight shot of the ball carrier after the whistle, a close-up of the scorer and his teammates running in after a touchdown, the coach after a turnover, a shot of the home crowd. The cameras never cover the start of a play: any shot ends the moment the ball is snapped. Minimal keeps only the big moments; Off is the plain live camera.
- **TV overlays** - game intro with a matchup card (both crests, records, key players) and a stadium and kickoff-temperature card; player stat lower-thirds on big plays (`#12 J. DOE - 18/24, 231 YDS, 2 TD`); drive summary (plays, yards, time of possession, result); team stat comparison; score update; two-minute warning; "next play" card on third and fourth downs and goal to go; penalty strip; a play-by-play ticker. Switch them off in Settings > Broadcast > TV overlays.
- **Halftime and postgame shows** - team comparison, top performers (passing, rushing, receiving, defense) and a highlight reel of the best plays of the half or game in slow motion. The postgame show adds the final score card, the player of the game and the box score, then returns to the menu. **A** goes to the next page, **B** skips the show.
- **Crowd and sideline** - on big plays for the home team the crowd jumps up in a wave around the stadium and cameras flash in the stands; coaches clap for touchdowns, takeaways and big sacks; after a touchdown the scorer's teammates run to him and cheer while the beaten defenders hang their heads.
- **First-person polish** - helmet facemask overlay (optional), head bob in step with your stride, the view leans toward the ball (in the air) and the ball carrier, on offense and defense, and a smaller scorebug.
- **Upscaler** option (Settings > Video): Off, DLSS or FSR. DLSS needs an NVIDIA RTX card; if your hardware cannot do it the game quietly keeps its normal upscaler.
- Settings now has four pages: Video, Gameplay, Broadcast, Audio.

## What to test / how to give feedback
This is the build to playtest. Please play a full game (try Cinematics Full, then Minimal, then Off) and tell me:
1. **Replays** - open Instant Replay a few times. Is the free camera comfortable (orbit, zoom, ground level, switching players)? Is the slow motion smooth? Does anything look wrong in the replay that looked right live (players frozen, ball in the wrong place)? Is the depth of field helping or in the way?
2. **Automatic replays** - too often, too rare, too long? Did A always skip?
3. **Camera director** - did any shot ever hide the start of a play, or hold on after you wanted to move on? Do the post-play cuts add to the game or slow it down?
4. **Overlays** - anything overlapping, unreadable or wrong (names, numbers, stat lines, drive yards, time of possession)? Too many cards?
5. **Halftime and postgame shows** - skippable when you want? The highlights - the right plays?
6. **First person** (offense and defense) - head bob too strong or too weak, is the facemask nice or annoying, does it look at the ball when it should?
7. **Performance** - note your GPU, resolution, quality tier and Upscaler setting, and when it dips (replays, shows, the crowd wave).
Send notes with the game version (0.5.0), what you were doing (quarter, down, which screen) and, for a visual bug, a screenshot. The autotest also writes screenshots to `Logs/autotest` if you run the game with `-pixAutotest -pixAutotestBroadcastOnly`.

## Known issues
- Players that were off screen (outside the camera's view) while a play was recorded can show a stale pose in a replay from another angle.
- The crowd wave moves whole sections of the stands together; fans do not bob one by one.
- Measured: on an RTX 5090 laptop at 2560x1440 (High) the autotest game runs at about 101 FPS average and 87 FPS 1%-low during live play, and 119 average during replays and shows.
- Referees are hidden during replays of older plays.
- DLSS and FSR depend on your hardware; on other cards the option keeps the normal upscaler.
- No commentary voice yet (the ticker text is there, a voice layer can plug in later).

## How to play
Download `PIXFootball_v0.5.0_Windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `PIXFootball.exe`. Plug in an Xbox controller before launching for the best experience. Requires Windows 10/11 64-bit and a DirectX 12 GPU.
