# PIX Football v0.10.0 - "Choreography"

**Plays are now planned before they happen.** This release answers your v0.9.0 playtest notes and nothing else (no College Network, no new modes). Running uses clean motion-capture instead of the old galloping takes, the defense follows its assignments, the quarterback walks through a real snap and drop, tackles and catches are booked ahead of time so bodies stop floating while an animation plays, kickoffs line up with the ball, the linemen and bodies were rebuilt, and there are many more of your Draft Fever Bowl animations in play.

## Running and movement
- Motion matching picks the pose each frame from the clean running set for the player's speed and direction; planted feet stay planted (contact slide at a run and sprint 0.07-0.10 m/s, was 0.24-0.30), the sole sits on the turf (error under 0.5 cm, was up to 2.4 cm hovering or sinking).
- Back-pedals and shuffles are planted too: back-pedal contact slide 0.11-0.17 m/s (was 0.6-1.2), shuffle 0.16 m/s (was 0.3-0.6).
- Engaged linemen: planted-foot slide down from 0.8-1.2 m/s to 0.03-0.17 m/s at the start of a block.
- Foot and step limits keep a landing foot inside the reach of the leg, so linemen stop over-stepping.

## Quarterback and kicks
- Full sequence: huddle break, walk, survey, step in, crouch, cadence, snap, secure (0.2 s), 3 / 5 / 7 step drop or shotgun gather, set, throw. The simulation fixes how long each part lasts and the body follows exactly.
- Planted-foot slide through the drop 0.04-0.06 m/s; the feet no longer cross.
- Kickoff, punt and field goal take their contact time from the simulation so the foot meets the ball.

## Choreography
- Tackles, sacks, falls and get-ups are booked ahead; the lead-in is played at the speed the player is really running. Planted-foot slide in action takes 1.16 -> 0.20 m/s on average (median 0).
- Receivers' hands meet the ball at the catch: ball-to-hand gap at the catch frame 0.57 m (v0.9.0) -> 0.10 m.
- Defensive backs' hip flips are planned, so a corner who turns to run no longer snaps.
- Sack, interception and big-gain celebrations play after the whistle.
- Chest rotation snaps in 60 plays: 3,724 -> 704.

## Defense and plays
- Rule-based coverage (zone drops, Cover 3 Match, man coverage with a rule for swaps), spies, blitz gaps and zone drops; every tuning number in one file.
- Read Option 2.97 -> 4.61 yards a carry, Outside Zone 3.38 -> 5.08, Draw 3.35 -> 4.08, punts average 45.9 yards.
- A 4,000-play check: rush 3.9 yards a play and pass 7.4 yards a dropback, completion 53 %, 213 sacks, 47 interceptions. Football-like, slightly lower than v0.9.0 (pass 7.6) because the new choreography and defense are tighter.

## Performance
Built player, 2560 x 1440 High, 80 s of live play on an RTX 5090 laptop: **148.5 FPS on average and 114.5 FPS for the slowest 1 %** (5 % low 126.5, minimum 35; GPU 6.3 ms, main thread 4.5 ms). v0.9.1 measured 145 / 82 on the same machine and v0.9.0 82 / 50, so the new motion matching, choreography and body layers cost nothing measurable. Short bursts around kickoffs can still show up as hitches if the PC is busy with other programs.

## Known issues
- **Wrap tackles** still slide a little at the start of the hit (planted-foot slide about 1.6 m/s mean, 0.7 median): the diving take drags its low foot over the turf. Falls and carrier tackles are better.
- **Jumping and get-up animations** are still played by the view, not by the simulation, so their feet can still slide (a few contacts per 60 plays).
- **Jogging and shuffling** still skate a little: mean slide 0.2-0.4 m/s (the median is fine, 0.1). Below about 1.7 m/s the older walk is used.
- **Sprinters are played 20-25 % faster than the fastest clean take we have**, so a top-speed sprint is the clip sped up. A real sprint capture would fix it.
- **Seat and sleeve skin** still stretch a little at extreme poses (a deep hip bend or an arm straight overhead); fixing it needs a hand-made corrective shape.
- **Grip and wrap hands** are slightly clawed.
- **Field goal kicks** pass about 17 cm from the ball (the take's leg is nearly straight at contact).
- **Under-center drops** take 8 landings for a 5-step drop; a coach would count more steps than the call says. Roll-outs and boots use the running animations with the ball at the ear.
- **Hand-offs and play-action fakes** still use the old pair takes; the pivot toward the runner is not choreographed. Interception and big-gain celebrations were checked in code, not yet seen in a replay.
- **Clip changes** per second are still above target for linemen (about 1.0 against a target of 0.5).
- **Menus are unchanged**: the 25 new UI directions are mockups only. Pick one and it will be built next.
- Everything listed under Known issues in v0.9.0 that is not about animation still applies.
