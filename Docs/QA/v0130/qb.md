## What was done (the design in one paragraph)
The BoNES / Yan quarterback takes are 10 s pieces of one continuous recording (he walks, turns and throws inside one clip) and the match **discards root motion** (probe `QbRootProbe`: hips travel 2.4 to 5 m in the clip, 0.1 to 0.24 m in the match), so a clip is not a unit. `QbClipCatalog` measures every QB clip (30 Hz, `QbClipAudit` + the ANIMLIB `ClipQa` row) and cuts **windows**: stable loops (stance, pocket), whole short clips (cadence), event windows around the catch of a snap and the release of a throw (peak of the faster hand), each with the planted-foot slide as the match would show it, the hips travel, the facing, the throwing hand, a 15 Hz hips path. The result is `PixQbClips.asset` (`QbClipTable`, Resources). `SimAgentView.QbMocap.cs` plays the windows: pre-snap stance (loop, new window every 3 to 6 s, no repeat), the count (a clip fitted between the start of the count and the start of the snap clip), the snap (clip started so its catch frame is the tick the sim completes the snap, ETA from the stage), the pocket (loops, pump fake), the throw (clip started on the sim's wind-up and **servoed every frame so its release frame is the tick the sim releases the ball**; held at the release pose if the sim waits for the body to align). Root travel of a window is given back as a capped (0.4 m) view offset and the sim's own sway / wind-up step is cancelled, so planted feet stay planted. Every window carries its own facing (`ActionAnimator.YawOverride`). The pose layer (QbSeq / QbThrow) stays as the fallback and still owns the drop, the hand-off, the play fake, scrambles / roll-outs (moving throws), kneel, spike, left-handers without a mirror clip.

## What the data said (why the drop is what it is)
* `YAN__QB_xStepDropback_Walk / _Settle` and `BGS__QB_xStepDropback_into_stop` are **lateral crossover shuffles** (travel heading -78 deg against the hips, feet cross), not back-pedals. They cannot play a straight drop; they fit a roll-out / boot (hips square, 3.8 m/s) exactly, and a 1.7 s Walk loops (7-step Walk, 3 cycles, seam 0).
* The straight drops are the `QB_undercenter_catch_to_N_step` takes (catch, pivot, back-pedal, plant): travel 2.9 m (3-step), 3.2 to 3.9 m (5-step), 7.2 m (7-step), at 1.4 to 3 m/s. The sim drops 3.4 / 5.5 / 7.6 m at 3 m/s. So the sim's drop point is moved along its line to the take's distance (hook `PlaySimulation.QbRefitDrop`, at most 40 percent, before the snap) and the take plays at its own scale (path scale 1, speed servoed to the sim's arrival, planted feet stay planted).
* Kneel (`BONES_QB_kneel`, hips never under 0.82 m), slide (`BONES_QB_slide`, min 0.69 m) and `QBSneak__*_A/_B` (static retarget, no motion) are bad data; no clean take exists in the inventory (only `CelebrationTouchdownKneelreverently`, not imported, not a snap-and-kneel). They stay on the pose layer / locomotion, and the catalog takes new names by rule (`QbClipCatalog.Fit.cs`: kneel, slide, spike, sneak, roll, fake, drop rules; a take whose actor never goes down or has no motion is rejected with the reason in `Logs/qb/catalog_rejects.csv`), so `QB_Sneak_00N_AB`, `QB_falling_throw_00N_AB`, `QB_cadence_00N_*` from BULK-2 are picked up at the next `BuildBatch`.
