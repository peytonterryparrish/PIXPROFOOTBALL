## 2. What was built

- **Bigger database (`MotionDbBuilder`).** `-pixMmList <file>` adds takes (`MOB1_*` Mobility Pro FBX, `S100_*` 100STYLE `.anim` slices, anything else a Mixamo FBX), `-pixMmDrop @file` removes base-set takes, `-pixMmPriorFile` gives a take a cost (quality prior, `MotionDb.Clip.prior`), `-pixMmNormFile` keeps the v0.12 normalisation of the 21 features (a hundred slow takes would otherwise shrink the velocity spread and with it the cost scale of every old take), `Spec.Pseudo` plays a slice that does not close (seam over 6 cm: nearly all 100STYLE cuts) like a loop (the matcher searches again at its end, the inertializer hides the seam; speed calibrated like a loop). `Logs/motion/db_clips.csv` is the per-clip table (speed, angle, seam, flight, pelvis facing, contacts). Mirror twins for everything (`_M`).
- **100STYLE root units.** The slices carry `RootT` in the units of the avatar they were converted on: on the match body the root ran 15 times too fast (27 m/s for a 1.9 m/s walk, hips 15 m up). `StyleRootFix` reads the factor from the clip itself (hips as high above the lowest planted sole as the legs are long; k = 0.068-0.073 over the 125 slices, root speed afterwards 1.87 m/s against the catalogue's 1.89) and scales `RootT.x/y/z` in the cleaned copy; the raw slices stay untouched in `Style100/Clips`.
- **Personal choice (`MotionStyle`, new).** Cost added to a candidate take in the matcher's search: a fixed 0..1.2 per take drawn from the player's id (a take and its mirror share it), 0.6 off the takes of his two favourite 100STYLE families (role-weighted draw: linemen Heavyset / Stiff / March, receivers and backs Roadrunner / Rocket / Strutting, backs and backers OnToes / BentKnees / Rushed), a recent-use cost shared by the whole stadium (0.3 per fresh entry, cap 4, decay 25 s) so near-equal takes are used in turn, and the per-take quality prior. The playing take keeps its personal cost but not the recent-use cost. A masked first search (a body leaving a stand) gets a personal phase jitter (0.04 per row), so teammates do not enter the cycle on the same frame. `-pixStyleOff` removes all of it; `-pixStyleSpread`, `-pixStyleFamily`, `-pixStyleRecent`, `-pixStyleSeedAdd`, `-pixStyleBan` for QA.
- **Speed fit stays in charge.** `WarpPenalty` 6 -> 20; a plan guard (a take that is too slow is judged against the average speed of the planned second: a body that accelerates leaves a slow take within a stride); the speed of a take is its mean, not that of a row (rows of a slice that does not close dip and spike); more than 1.6x too slow costs 40 (r - 1.6)^2 more and more than 2.2x is out (+3000): the style slices walk at 1-2.5 m/s and must not play a run. `-pixMmWarpPen`, `-pixMmNoPlanGuard`.
- **Tighter stride warp.** Speed filter 0.035 -> 0.015 s, warp filter 0.045 -> 0.020 s once a take has played 0.25 s: at 7 m/s2 the old 0.08 s lag was a planted foot sliding 0.5 m/s while the body accelerated (`-pixMmSpeedTau`, `-pixMmWarpTau`; `-pixMmStay` / `-pixMmDwell` kept for QA at the v0.10 values).
- **Cadence.** `NaturalStyle.TempoSpread` 0.14 -> 0.22 (tempo 0.875-1.125); the stride warp takes the difference back, so ground speed stays the simulation's (`-pixTempoSpread`).
- **Idle micro-motion (`IdleLife`, `IdleLifeData`, `SimAgentView.IdleLife`, new).** A floor of life for any body that stands without moving: it watches the pose it is given (the animator rewrites every bone each frame, so an unchanged pose is a held pose) and, while chest, head and upper arms have not turned for a moment (< 4 deg/s summed, low-passed 0.5 s), fades in micro-motion above the pelvis (feet and pelvis untouched): the motion of real standing mocap, baked from the Mobility Pro `Stand_Relaxed_Idle`, `Idle_v2` and `Fgt_v1..v4` takes (`IdleLifeBuilder`: turn of 11 bones against their own mean, degrees, 30 fps, `PixIdleLife.asset`), a take, speed (0.88-1.14) and amplitude (0.75-1.25) of his own, every take scaled to 1.6 deg rms (a fidget that looks around by 45 degrees becomes a sway of a few), every bone capped (spine 3, chest 4, head 8, shoulders 4, upper arms 9, forearms 12, hands 16 deg), ping-pong playback (no seam). Arms stay put for a quarterback, a carrier, a hand-down stance, a block, a catch, a throw; contact takes and ragdolls are skipped. Procedural rhythms from his `NaturalStyle` when the asset is missing (`-pixIdleLifeProcedural`). One-line hook at the end of `SimAgentView.LateUpdate`; `-pixIdleLifeOff` switches it off.
- **QA tools.** `IdleProbe` (`-pixIdleProbe`, self-contained: runs inside `EngineAudit` and `MatchWatchAudit` without a hook); `EngineAudit` csv gets `styleRate`, `mmCost`; `-pixMmSelLog` gets `meanCost`; `LocoShots mmtake` writes `mmtake.csv`, takes `-pixLocoMinSpeed` and plays the non-closing slices; `MobilityCurveStrip` also strips `Assets/_Licensed/Style100` (by curve NAME).

## 3. How the takes were chosen

Candidates (217 takes, 476 clips with mirrors): 70 Mobility Pro (19 loops incl. diagonals / back-pedal strafes / circles, 30 starts from standing, 13 stops, 4 90-degree cuts, 4 180 turns), 125 100STYLE slices (95 loops of >= 0.5 s at >= 0.9 m/s, 30 starts that end at >= 1.3 m/s), 22 Mixamo loops the v0.12 build left out; plus the 22 base takes.
1. **Plays**: three 36-play `EngineAudit` runs per round with a deliberately large personal spread (2.0, then 1.2), so every candidate is played in real conditions. Per take: stances (>= 3 planted frames), minimum slide (audit rule), flight (both toes > 10 cm), in the take's own speed band (0.75-1.3 x its speed), with the matcher's contact tag also down (`clipqa3.awk`).
2. **Treadmill** (`LocoShots mmtake`, 153 loop / pseudo-loop takes): calibration trims (`natural2_calib.csv` = the v0.12 table + 61 new rows) and a second opinion for takes with no stances in play.
3. **Tails.** The mean slide is carried by a few stances above 1 m/s, so every take is also held to its tail over all speeds it plays at (`tails.awk`): more than 5.5 % of its stances above 1 m/s (>= 10 stances), or a mean over 0.45, drops it. This removed the Mixamo strafes / diagonals and `Run_Treadmill_Running` (an in-place clip, no root motion: believed speed 0.16 m/s, played at 3-4 m/s) that had passed rounds 1 and 2, and every slow loop (< 0.95 m/s).
Round 1 kept 136 of 238 takes, round 2 119 of 136, the tail rounds 91 (18 base + 73 new): **182 clips**. Dropped for good: every Mobility stop / cut / 180 take (stance slide mean 0.3-2.0 m/s over 5-67 stances; the v0.12 finding holds), `Sprint_Standard` and `Run_Two_Cycle_Sprint` (sprint flight 0.30 / 0.62), the v0.12 workhorses `Jog_Runningk` and `Run_Jog_Forward` (stance slide p50 0.42 / 0.29), the bouncy Mixamo runs (flight 34-56 %). Priors (`natural2_prior.csv`, 75 takes): 0.4-1.5 by measured quality, 1.0 for takes with no in-play data.

## 4. Definitions

- **Jog planted slide** (3-4.5 m/s, sim action Run): `D:\PIXFootball\Work\AnimLib\stance_clip.awk` (the v0.12 definition) on `motion_frames.csv` of the 36-play `EngineAudit`: a stance = >= 3 consecutive frames in which the audit's geometric rule calls one foot planted (sole 4 cm to enter, 7 cm to leave); its slide = the MINIMUM speed of that foot over the stance; mean over stances that end at 3-4.5 m/s. (a) as is; (b) `slide_mm.awk`: only stances played entirely by the matcher (the rest are action clips of other tracks: hand-offs, shed moves, `Jog_to_Jog_135`); (c) `slide_tag.awk`: only frames in which the matcher's own contact tag also says down (a swing foot that skims the turf is flagged planted by the audit's geometry and carries part of the mean). The v0.12 figure of 0.18 is not reproduced on this tree and play set: the baseline gives 0.242 / 0.259 in two runs (noise between runs of one configuration with another take mix is +-0.03).
- **Neighbours in step**: `NaturalProbe` (unchanged): frames in which two same-group neighbours (< 20 m, both > 3 m/s, >= 1 s together) are within 0.10 cycle of each other, chance 20 %.
- **Per-player cadence sd**: `cadence.awk`: the cadence factor (`MotionMatcher.StyleRate`, logged per frame as `styleRate`) of every run (an agent's consecutive matcher frames at 5.5-7 m/s, >= 30 frames); sd over the runs. v0.12 reported 0.054 with the same definition.
- **Still standing bodies** (`IdleProbe`): 2 s windows of bodies whose root moved < 0.25 m; 14 key bones against the first frame of the window in the avatar's frame; "no pose change" = no bone turned 3 deg and the hips moved < 3 cm (also given at 1.5 deg / 1.5 cm, which hardly any body fails: held mocap poses breathe by a degree). Windows inside contact takes / ragdolls / culled off-screen bodies / with a clip change are left out. Baseline = the same 14 plays of the same seed, `D:\PIXFootball\Renders\v0130_natural2\mw_base` (2,019 windows).
- **Sprint flight**: `FormProbe` row "Sprint >=5.5 m/s", share of floating frames that are flight (both toes > 10 cm).
- **New matcher clips selected**: names with system `MotionMatcher` in the run's `clips_played.csv` that are not in the baseline's (`newclips.sh`; list `D:\PIXFootball\Work\v013\new_clips_natural.txt`).

## W2.3 Chest pops

The probe (`ChestPopProbe`, `-pixChestProbe`: every frame in which the world rotation of the chest bone exceeds 1400 deg/s, with action, clip, layer, trigger, and whether the pelvis or the chest turns) on the EngineAudit and on a 24-play game: the pops are NOT the matcher's switches (4 % of them): they are authored block / hit takes (`Engaged`, about 60 %) and whole-body snaps at the stance pose (`Stance`, clip change, pelvis turns with the chest: the five linemen at the same frame at 2,500 deg/s). So the fix is a cap on the trunk, not fewer takes: `SpineRate` (new, `Loco/SpineRate.cs`): the chest of the final pose (the last thing in `LateUpdate`, called from `IdleLifeLate`: no new hook) may turn at most 1,150 deg/s against the chest it showed the frame before; the excess is taken off the SPINE bone (the whole trunk turns about the lower spine, the chest keeps its pose against the spine, arms and head follow), so a pop becomes a 2-4 frame swing. No cap while contact-driven, ragdoll, tackled / fallen / get-up, on the first frame, or when the whole body was moved (> 1 m) or turned (> 25 deg) between two frames. Head / chest / pelvis LOOK limits (`NaturalBody.cs`) untouched. QA: `-pixSpineRateOff`, `-pixSpineRate <deg/s>`. Result: 399 -> 40 in the game (20 against 106 in the 60-play A / B). The probe itself still counts about 900 frames over 1,400 deg/s in the game (it has no `ContactDriven` / ground-action exemptions and counts frames in which the whole body turns); the audit's rule counts 40.
