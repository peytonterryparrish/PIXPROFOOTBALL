## Plain-English summary for the owner (the 15 worst)

I watched three full CPU-vs-CPU games on the broadcast camera, one in each of clear / rain / fog weather, in day, dusk and night. I also checked your v0.10.0 build with its own screenshots.

1. **Your "half bodies / floating / whole team disappears on kickoff" is one bug, found.** When a player is outside the camera picture the game stops animating him but keeps nudging his hips, so he sinks into the turf at about 3 m a second. After 4 seconds he is 12 m underground; the worst was 29 m. In one full game 15 % of all player-time was spent like that, on every kickoff and every long play the camera follows (B-001, B-002).
2. **Buried players stay buried** even when the camera comes back, because a sunk body is never counted as visible again. He is missing until the next play (B-003).
3. **Returners and some linemen are upside-down:** the kick returner shows his legs folded behind his back on every return (B-004), and the centre does a handstand over the ball on some snaps (B-005).
4. **Offensive linemen stand up** instead of getting in a 3-point stance on about 4 snaps in 5, hands on thighs while the defensive line is down (B-014).
5. **The button helper bar at the bottom cannot be hidden** and is 57-80 % of the screen wide (B-008).
6. **The snap comes 0.9-1.9 seconds after the play starts:** no huddle, no shifts, no time for the play clock (B-006; the working tree already changes this, re-test).
7. **The offence cannot score:** 0-7 and 0-0 (after a full overtime) in two 36-play games; the pass block loses to the rush every time within a second, with almost no sacks (B-007).
8. **The camera loses the ball on every kick** (the ball goes above the top of the picture), flips from one sideline to the other (88 m cut) between plays, and keeps the players tiny (about 50-60 px tall at 1080p).
9. **Every score is shown three times at once** (banner, score card, scorebug) and the TOUCHDOWN / TACKLED banner covers the line of scrimmage; the same tackle is announced twice.
10. **Bodies snap:** linemen and defensive backs have rotations of thousands of degrees per second for single frames; the DL "get-off" is the worst.
11. **Feet hover:** 9-21 cm off the turf in blocks, both feet off the ground in 17-33 % of running frames.
12. **Feet skate:** a planted foot should not move, but linemen, receivers and kickers slide 1.6-2.8 m/s (median).
13. **Game clock:** 22-28 s are burned on every snap (also after incompletions, during the two-minute drill); the CPU never calls a timeout and the trailing team gets no last play or spike.
14. **Players overlap and the referee stands inside the play** (bodies 0.05-0.26 m apart, referee within 0.5 m of the returner).
15. **Sound is thin:** only whistles, tackles, catches, kicks and a few cheers ever play; no snap, no cleats, no blocks, no throws, no crowd reactions to sacks.

## How this was made

- Tool: `MatchWatchAudit` (`Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/Tools/MatchWatchAudit*.cs`, flag `-pixMatchWatch`, private copy `D:\PIXFootball\Work\Audit\PIX`). It starts a real CPU-vs-CPU match through `GameSession.StartMatch` with the shipped MatchDirector, PlayStage, broadcast camera and HUD, runs 60 Hz with a fixed frame time, and checks every frame: renderers drawn per body, missing LOD parts, skinned bounds, bodies sunk or floating, planted-foot slide, rotation pops, body-vs-sim facing, overlaps, ball vs hands, camera framing, plus stance snapshots at every snap, audio clip starts, play-by-play, and HUD element rectangles at 1920x1080 and 2560x1440. A 60-frame ring of broadcast-camera frames is dumped as 6-tile film strips around every sim event, and each detector hit stores a broadcast frame plus a close-up of the culprit.
- Run: `Unity -batchmode -projectPath <private copy> -logFile <log> -executeMethod PixFootball.EditorTools.MatchWatchAudit.Run -pixMatchWatch -pixMwOut <dir> [-pixMwHome id -pixMwAway id -pixMwQuarter 3 -pixMwSeed n -pixMwWeather 0-5 -pixMwTime 0-3 -pixMwCine 0|1|2 -pixMwMaxPlays n -pixMwUser 1]`. A 3-minute-quarter game takes about 25 minutes. Not for the shared editor.
- Games (committed v0.10.0 code): g1 Buffalo v Miami, clear, night, seed 110110 (33 plays, 0-7); g2 Seattle v Dallas, rain, day, seed 220220 (36 plays incl. overtime, 0-0); g3 Chicago v Green Bay, fog, dusk, Cinematics Full, seed 330330 (31 plays, 7-0). Data and images: `D:\PIXFootball\Renders\match_watch_v0100\{g1,g2,g3}` (`events.log`, `plays.txt`, `summary.txt`, `issues.txt`, `stance_snapshots.csv`, `strips\`, `anomaly\`, `hud\`), real-player screenshots in `...\exe_ref`.
- Rows come from the measurements above, from looking at the images, and from code reading by five reviewers (each code-only claim says "code read" and cites file:line). Rows I could not confirm were dropped.

## Caveats the tracks must know

- The shared tree already has uncommitted v0.11 edits (culling mode, pre-snap tempo, HUD pop-up lane, watch hint guard, camera, flow). The rows describe v0.10.0; where a row says "tree:" or "already changes", re-measure before fixing. Re-run the tool on the integrated tree: the sink rows (B-001 to B-003) must go to zero (`CULL_OFFSCREEN_BODY_SINKS_UNDER_TURF_*`, `VIS_BURIED_BODY_ROOT_IN_VIEW_*` in `issues.txt`).
- Games g1 and g2 were run with the editor's saved Cinematics = Off (director shots, replays, intro not covered); g3 used Cinematics Full. g1 / g2 have no stance snapshots (a tool bug fixed before g3).
- Perf is not measured: the harness renders extra frames, so frame-time hitches in `issues.txt` are not game hitches. Use the earlier perf notes.
- g3 (fog, dusk) renders look very dark in the harness (auto-exposure does not settle for off-screen captures): no row relies on brightness. `VIS_VIEW_INACTIVE` / `VIS_TEAM_MISSING` lines in g3 are the auto-replay hiding the live bodies, not a bug.
- `Mask` in the HUD files is a probe artefact. The HUD agent's rows are watch-mode (bottom bar) from the harness and user-mode (top-left bug) from the real player screenshots.
