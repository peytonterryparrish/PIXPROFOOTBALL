## Higher-end pass beyond the rows

- **Players at LOD0 where you look.** Size-based LOD (E-404) plus a shadow proxy: while a body is LOD0 (60 k triangles) it is drawn for the camera only and its shadow is cast by the LOD1 mesh (28 k, same silhouette within 0.7 percent) drawn as shadows-only (`PhotoPlayer.ShadowProxy`).
- **Shadows.** 4 cascades tuned to the 20-50 m player range, PCSS filtering, lower sun, floodlight rim shadows on High, foot blobs on every tier.
- **Edges.** TAA tuned, DLSS Quality by default on RTX hardware, anisotropic filtering forced on from Medium (16x on High / Ultra) for the turf stripes, the lines and the jersey weave.
- **Turf.** Blade detail now reaches the 20-45 m the players are seen from (it faded out at 14-55 m), a 60 cm clump scale of light / dark grass and a stronger blade normal.
- **Grade.** Cleaner live image (no grain, less vignette and bloom), motion blur 0.18, far-only depth of field, a cloud layer.
- **Numbers.** Jersey numerals 20 / 25 percent larger.

## Measurements

Setup: Unity editor (not a built player; no builds in this track), Game view 2560 x 1440, tier High, clear day (`-pixWeather 1 -pixTime 1`), one 1-minute-quarter match (`-pixAutotestQuarter 1 -pixAutotestSpeed 1.5`), RTX 5090 Laptop, DX12, native TAA (DLSS is not applied in the editor unless `-pixUpscaler`, and measured slower there, so the editor numbers do not show the upscaler win). The baseline is git HEAD in the same private copy.

| run | avg fps | 5 % low | 1 % low | CPU / GPU ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline (HEAD), full match | 125.8 | 75.8 | 63.0 | 8.5 / 6.0 |
| after, full match (final) | 95.1 | 61.6 | 51.0 | 10.7 / 8.1 |
| baseline (HEAD), 80 s of live play, x2 | 136.3 / 136.6 | 87.3 / 87.4 | 74.8 / 75.4 | 7.0 / 5.6 |
| after, 80 s of live play, x2 | 99.7 / 94.3 | 67.3 / 56.6 | 41.0 / 51.4 | 9.3-9.8 / 7.5-7.8 |

So the look pass costs about 2.1 ms of GPU time and 2.2 ms of CPU-side time per frame in the editor (about 24 percent fps in the full match). Where it goes (single-feature switch-offs on the 94 fps build, run-to-run noise about 2-3 fps): player LOD edges (the whole screen-size LOD, against the old distances) 17 fps at the first edges, still the largest item; SSGI on High 8-11 fps (removed again from High); 4 cascades out to 110 m 5-8 fps (3 cascades on High); depth of field about 2 fps; PCSS filtering, shadow proxy, foot blobs, anisotropic 16x, TAA high, SSAO, contact shadows, motion blur, cloud layer: 0-1 fps each (below the noise); the sum of all small ones is about 1.6 ms. DLSS Quality in the editor (flag `-pixUpscaler 3`) was slower (70-74 fps): the editor path is not representative.
The v0.7 built-player numbers on the same machine were 107 avg / 70 1 % low (`Docs/QA/v070/SUMMARY.md`); the same relative cost on top of that would be about 80-85 avg and 55-60 for the 1 % low: **the ">= 90 avg / >= 70 1 %-low" target is NOT demonstrated** here (no player build allowed). Levers if the player build misses it, in order of visible loss: LOD edges 400 / 200 px (+9 fps), depth of field off (+2), TAA quality medium, DLSS Quality (the Auto default cuts the shaded pixels by a third in the player; not measurable in the editor).
