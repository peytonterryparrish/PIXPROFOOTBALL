## Fixed at integration
- 175 `CinemachineBrain.ManualUpdate` errors per game (`MatchCameraRig.SetOverride`): the brain is switched to manual mode for the one update (0 in the integration game).
- The Mobility clips carried an Animator "Speed" curve that took over the locomotion Speed parameter (8.8 M warnings a game): `MobilityCurveStrip` removes controller-parameter curves only (32 clips; the motion database was rebuilt with MOBILITY's exact settings first, see `INTEGRATION_NOTES.md`).
- Bodies mis-faced (up to 95 deg) and not set at the snap on short pre-snaps (tries, kickoffs, hurry-up; 187 of 4,158 snapshots, baseline 0), caused by the new timing: the play clock's 8 s floor forced the snap while bodies walked in. RULES follow-up: 16 s floor, a watched game holds the snap until the stage has been set for 0.8 s, the count waits for a shift or motion to settle (up to 2.5 s), the motion man is at rest 0.9 s before the snap. 40-play re-check: 0 of 880 mis-faced, 0 toes up; whistle to snap 26.3 s.
