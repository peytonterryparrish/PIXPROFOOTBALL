## 4. What changed, and why the fouls are causal now

All of `TryPreSnapPenalty` (about 95 lines of dice at the snap: a roll against `0.0185 x (1.55 - awareness)` etc.) is gone. In its place (`Sim/Rules/PreSnapFouls.cs`, `Core/Rules/PreSnapRules.cs`):

- **Legality from the alignment** (`PreSnapRules.Analyze`, pure, rule-set aware): on the line = hips within `RuleSet.OnLineDepthYards` (0.6) of the ball (the formations put linemen at 0.15, receivers on the line at 0 and "off" at 1.0; the old validator's 1.05 counted a flanker a yard off as on the line); at least 7 on it (`MinOnLine`), at most 4 off it (`MaxBackfield`); eligible = an eligible position that is off the line, or on the line AND an end of it (a receiver with a teammate on the line outside him is `Covered`, so ineligible); the quarterback and the linemen never; an ineligible position at an end of the line is an illegal formation (numbering). The culprit is the off-the-line player nearest the line (the one who should have been on it) or the ineligible end. The simulation feeds it the real positions at the snap (`PlaySimulation.OffenseAlignment()`). All 22 offensive formations pass for both rule sets (test). `IsReceiverEligible` itself is unchanged this pass (the covered tight end of an Ace set is still targeted; see Known issues).
- **Legality from the movement** (`PreSnapRules.CheckMovement`): one man in motion (`MaxMenInMotion`), not moving toward the line faster than `MotionForwardTolerance` (0.45 yd/s) at the snap; after a shift (two or more players changing place together) everybody still for `ShiftSetSeconds` (1.0 s). The sim tracks when every body last moved and which ones shifted together.
- **Early movers** (false start, offside, neutral zone infraction, encroachment): `DrawJumps` decides, when the snap count begins, who leaves early and how long before the snap (0.10 to 0.60 s). Chance per player = base x position weight (lineman 1, receiver on the line 0.8, back 0.35, QB 0.2; defensive lineman 1, linebacker on the line 0.4) x lack of awareness ((1.6 - awareness/99)/0.9, squared) x cadence length x fatigue x crowd (the away offense 1.2) x hard count (the defense bites x2.6 when the offense calls one: 5 % of snaps, 40 % in short yardage; the offense itself x0.8). The body then really lurches out of its stance (`JumpStep`: up to 4.2 yd/s for the offense, 5.5 for the defense) from `EarlyMoveAt`: an offensive player out of his stance for 0.2 s is a false start; a defender whose front edge enters the neutral zone (`PreSnapRules.InNeutralZone`, the ball's length) is classified by what happens there: the man across flinches (`NeutralZoneReact` x lack of awareness) = neutral zone infraction, bodies touch (lateral overlap and `EncroachContact`) = encroachment, else offside. A jump that starts too late to cross (or to reach 0.2 s) before the snap is just a quick get-off: no flag. A defender in the zone at the snap (a user lineman who creeps) is offside from the alignment alone.
- **Illegal formation**: `ApplyLineupMistakes` (a receiver or lineman of the line stands a yard off it: late substitution or wrong spot, awareness-weighted, 0.0007 per player on the line) and the legality check at the snap does the rest (a set that had exactly 7 on the line and now has 6 is flagged; one that had 8 is not).
- **Illegal shift / motion** (`PlanTheatre`, CPU offense with the count): a shift (two receivers squeeze toward each other, 4 to 7 % of snaps) is followed by the one second set plus the quarterback's patience (`ShiftGapMean` 0.55 s, spread x lack of awareness): a hasty quarterback snaps inside the second and draws the illegal shift, computed from the time the shifters really stopped. The man in motion (30 % of passes, 20 % of runs) can turn upfield early in the last 0.3 s (`MotionDrift`) and is flagged because his velocity toward the line at the snap is over the tolerance, or a second receiver is sent as well (`MotionBoth`) and two men move at the snap. This also gives the CPU the pre-snap motion, shift and blitz disguise that v0.11 listed as missing (B-031); the `Cadence` option does the same for headless games.
- **Delay of game**: a quarterback is slow (`SlowSnap` x lack of awareness x play complexity: motion, shift, hot route) and the play clock runs out first; the sim flags it at the clock's expiry. In the director the clock is the real one (`Sim.BeginCount(cadence, playClockLeft)`); a game the user plays keeps its own delay-of-game path.
- **View hook** (no change in other tracks' files): `PlaySimulation.EarlyMoverId` / `EarlyMoveAt` (pre-snap time the culprit started) / `PlannedSnapAt` / `HardCount` / `PreSnapFoul`. The culprit's sim body physically moves and the stage already follows the sim (a lunge out of the stance with `AgentAction.Run`), so the movement is on screen before the whistle; a dedicated flinch pose is a request (below). Tested in the sim (the culprit's position moves toward the line by more than 0.15 yd before the flag, 0.15 s or more before it); the stage playback was not captured on camera (no flagged play in the 40-play run fell inside a strip).
- **Director**: `MatchDirector.CadenceWindow` asks the sim for the count (`BeginCount` returns how long the quarterback really takes: motion, shift and set, a slow snap), now also runs `PresentFrame` and the score bug while it lasts (the old 0.6 to 1.0 s window ran no camera or HUD), and ends the moment the sim blows the play dead. Punts and field goals have no count in the live flow (only the headless sim gives them jumps).
- **Rule data** (`RuleSet`): `OnLineDepthYards`, `MinOnLine`, `MaxBackfield`, `ShiftSetSeconds`, `MovingSpeed`, `MaxMenInMotion`, `MotionForwardTolerance`, `NeutralZoneYards`; the pro and college sets agree on all of them (their fouls differ in yardage and clock as before). `PenaltyType` + `IllegalShift`, `IllegalMotion`, `NeutralZoneInfraction` (5 yards, dead ball; `Penalty.All` is 22).

Probe (not a calibration): `-pixPreSnapJump 14` multiplies the jump chances (`PreSnapTuning` static constructor). An 8-play `MatchWatchAudit` with it (`D:\PIXFootball\Renders\match_watch_v0120\rules_probe`) produced two offsides through the director flow ("pre-snap foul" -> dead -> `Offside accepted (cpu)` -> next play, no exception), whistle to snap median 26.1 s. In the confirming run one pre-snap foul fell in the 40 plays (illegal formation, play 38; the other flag was roughing the passer).

Side effect to know about: `view-vs-sim position error` rises (probe run: median 2.8 m, was 0.7 m) because the bodies now stand in the huddle for up to 14 s while the simulated agents already stand in formation; the audit's `SYNC_VIEW_FAR_FROM_SIM_1M` (595 over 40 plays, 30 of 40 plays) and `FACE_BODY_70DEG_OFF_SIM_FACING_STANDING` rules need the "walking / in the huddle" guard the baseline note asks for (CONTACT / the audit owner); they are not a stance defect.
